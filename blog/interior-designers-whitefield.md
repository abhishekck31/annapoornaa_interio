## Red Flags: What to Avoid When Hiring Interior Designers in Whitefield

Not all designers are created equal. Watch out for:

🚩 **No Portfolio or Website**
- Professional designers have a strong online presence
- If they can't show work, that's concerning

🚩 **Pressure to Decide Immediately**
- Good designers are in demand (they're not desperate)
- You should feel free to compare before committing

🚩 **Vague Pricing or Hidden Costs**
- Transparent pricing is a green flag
- Ask for detailed cost breakdowns upfront
- Be suspicious of "we'll adjust costs later"

🚩 **No References or Reviews**
- Real designers have happy clients
- Ask for contact details of previous clients in Whitefield
- Poor or no reviews online? Walk away

🚩 **Dismissing Your Ideas**
- Your home, your vision
- A good designer collaborates, not dictates
- If they're arrogant in consultations, that won't change

🚩 **No Written Agreement**
- Everything should be in writing: scope, cost, timeline, warranty
- Verbal promises mean nothing when disputes arise
